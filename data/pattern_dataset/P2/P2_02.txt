[m=4] Alice: We should also invest in green hydrogen production facilities powered by renewable energy to decarbonize sectors that are difficult to electrify. This policy could include grants for research and development as well as subsidies for early adopters to accelerate deployment.

This policy could mandate smart energy management systems in new constructions and offer subsidies for retrofitting existing buildings.
[m=6] Alice: We should establish a national carbon pricing mechanism that sets a clear price on carbon emissions to incentivize the adoption of low-carbon technologies and reduce overall emissions.
