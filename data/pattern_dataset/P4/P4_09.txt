topic: How should we design an energy policy that balances stable energy supply with the realization of a decarbonized society?
opening_hint: electric vehicle infrastructure and transportation electrification

[m=1] Alice: To accelerate transportation electrification, the government should implement a nationwide program that subsidizes the installation of fast-charging stations in all urban and rural areas, ensuring accessibility for all citizens. Additionally, mandating electric vehicle-ready infrastructure in all new residential and commercial buildings will support long-term adoption and reduce range anxiety.
[m=2] Alice: To further support decarbonization, the government should introduce incentives for public transportation systems to transition to electric buses by 2030, reducing urban emissions significantly. Additionally, establishing a national standard for battery recycling will ensure sustainable management of electric vehicle components and minimize environmental impact.
[m=3] Bob: I believe that to balance stable energy supply with decarbonization, the government should incentivize decentralized renewable energy generation, such as rooftop solar panels with battery storage, to reduce grid strain and enhance resilience. Additionally, implementing a policy that offers tax credits for households and businesses installing such systems would accelerate adoption and support a more stable, clean energy future.
[m=4] Bob: I think the government should establish a national grid modernization program that integrates smart grid technologies to optimize energy distribution and reduce losses. Additionally, mandating real-time energy consumption monitoring for large consumers would encourage efficient usage and support grid stability while advancing decarbonization goals.
[m=5] Carol: I propose the government implement a mandatory energy efficiency certification program for all industrial facilities, requiring regular audits and upgrades to reduce energy waste. This policy would ensure stable energy demand while promoting decarbonization through continuous improvement in industrial energy use.
[m=6] Carol: The government should establish a nationwide demand response program that incentivizes consumers to reduce or shift their electricity usage during peak hours through dynamic pricing. This policy would enhance grid stability and support decarbonization by optimizing energy consumption patterns without compromising supply reliability.
