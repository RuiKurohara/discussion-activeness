topic: How should we design an energy policy that balances stable energy supply with the realization of a decarbonized society?
opening_hint: green hydrogen production and industrial use

[m=1] Alice: I believe we should implement a policy that incentivizes the development of green hydrogen production facilities powered by renewable energy sources, specifically targeting heavy industries like steel and chemical manufacturing. This policy could include subsidies for renewable-powered electrolysis plants and mandates for industrial sectors to gradually increase their green hydrogen usage by set percentages over the next decade.
[m=2] Alice: To ensure a stable energy supply while advancing decarbonization, I propose establishing a national grid integration program that prioritizes energy storage technologies like large-scale batteries and pumped hydro. This program would provide grants and technical support to utilities to deploy these storage solutions, smoothing out renewable energy intermittency and securing consistent power delivery.
[m=3] Bob: I propose implementing a nationwide demand response program that incentivizes consumers to reduce or shift their electricity usage during peak times, thereby enhancing grid stability and reducing reliance on fossil fuel backup plants. This program should include real-time pricing signals and subsidies for smart home energy management systems to encourage active participation.
[m=4] Bob: I suggest establishing a comprehensive energy efficiency certification program for all new buildings, mandating strict standards that reduce energy consumption and carbon emissions. This policy should be supported by government incentives for retrofitting existing structures to meet these standards within the next decade.
[m=5] Carol: I think we should implement a policy that promotes urban vertical farming powered by renewable energy to reduce food transportation emissions and overall energy demand. This would include subsidies for vertical farm installations in cities and incentives for integrating renewable energy systems into these farms to support both decarbonization and stable energy consumption.
[m=6] Carol: I propose establishing a policy that supports the development of community-based microgrids powered by renewable energy to enhance local energy resilience and reduce transmission losses. This policy would include government grants for microgrid projects and training programs for local technicians to ensure sustainable operation and maintenance.
