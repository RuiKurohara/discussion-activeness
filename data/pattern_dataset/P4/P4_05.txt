topic: How should we design an energy policy that balances stable energy supply with the realization of a decarbonized society?
opening_hint: a carbon border tax and industrial decarbonization incentives

[m=1] Alice: Implementing a carbon border tax should include a transparent rebate system for domestic industries that invest in green technologies to maintain competitiveness. Additionally, the government should establish a fund that provides direct subsidies to industries adopting electrification and hydrogen fuel to accelerate industrial decarbonization.

This policy would help balance intermittent renewable energy sources and maintain grid reliability during peak demand periods.

This policy would enhance local energy resilience and reduce reliance on centralized fossil fuel plants by incentivizing community-level energy storage and real-time consumption adjustments.
[m=4] Bob: I propose that the government should incentivize the development of large-scale offshore wind farms paired with comprehensive grid modernization efforts. This policy would ensure a stable energy supply by harnessing abundant offshore resources while upgrading transmission infrastructure to support decarbonization goals effectively.

This policy would enhance grid flexibility and reduce the need for fossil fuel backup plants by leveraging smart meters and real-time pricing.
[m=6] Carol: The government should establish a mandatory energy efficiency certification program for all new buildings, requiring them to meet strict decarbonization and energy stability standards. This policy would reduce overall energy demand and ensure that urban development supports a reliable, low-carbon energy system.
