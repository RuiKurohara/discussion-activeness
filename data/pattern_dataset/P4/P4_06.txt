topic: How should we design an energy policy that balances stable energy supply with the realization of a decarbonized society?
opening_hint: nationwide building energy-efficiency retrofits

[m=1] Alice: To enhance nationwide building energy-efficiency retrofits, I propose implementing a mandatory energy audit and retrofit certification program for all commercial and residential buildings over 20 years old. This policy would ensure systematic upgrades and provide incentives for property owners to improve insulation, heating, and cooling systems, accelerating our progress toward a decarbonized society.
[m=2] Alice: To ensure a stable energy supply while advancing decarbonization, I propose establishing a national grid-scale energy storage fund that supports the deployment of large-capacity batteries and pumped hydro storage. This fund would provide grants and low-interest loans to accelerate storage projects, enhancing grid reliability and enabling higher integration of renewable energy sources.
[m=3] Bob: I propose implementing a nationwide smart grid system that dynamically balances energy demand and supply using AI-driven analytics to optimize renewable energy usage and reduce waste. This system would include real-time monitoring and incentives for consumers to shift their energy usage to off-peak times, enhancing grid stability while promoting decarbonization.
[m=4] Bob: I suggest introducing a government-backed program to incentivize the development and deployment of green hydrogen production facilities using renewable energy. This would provide a stable, clean energy source for industries and power generation, supporting both energy security and decarbonization goals.
[m=5] Carol: I believe that to balance stable energy supply with decarbonization, we should implement a nationwide program to retrofit public transportation fleets with electric and hydrogen-powered vehicles. This policy would reduce urban emissions and ensure reliable, clean transit options while stimulating green technology industries.
[m=6] Carol: I think we should establish a national program to incentivize the development of community-based microgrids powered by renewable energy and integrated with smart demand management. This would enhance local energy resilience and reduce transmission losses while supporting decarbonization efforts.
