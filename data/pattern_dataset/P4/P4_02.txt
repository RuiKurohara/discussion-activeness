topic: How should we design an energy policy that balances stable energy supply with the realization of a decarbonized society?
opening_hint: restarting or extending nuclear power plants

[m=1] Alice: I believe we should implement a phased restart of existing nuclear power plants with strict safety upgrades and transparent community involvement to ensure public trust. Additionally, establishing a government-backed fund to support local economies affected by plant operations would help balance energy stability with social responsibility.
[m=2] Alice: I propose that we accelerate investment in smart grid technologies to enhance energy distribution efficiency and better integrate renewable energy sources. This approach will stabilize supply while advancing decarbonization by enabling real-time demand management and reducing energy waste.
[m=3] Bob: We should implement a nationwide energy storage incentive program to encourage the adoption of large-scale battery systems, which will help stabilize the grid during peak demand and renewable intermittency. This policy will ensure a reliable energy supply while accelerating the transition to a decarbonized society by maximizing renewable energy utilization.
[m=4] Bob: We should establish a national program to retrofit existing buildings with energy-efficient technologies, reducing overall energy demand and supporting decarbonization efforts. This policy will stabilize energy consumption patterns and lower emissions by improving building insulation, lighting, and heating systems.
[m=5] Carol: I think we should implement a nationwide program to incentivize urban vertical farming using renewable energy, which would reduce food transportation emissions and stabilize local energy demand. This policy would promote decarbonization by integrating sustainable agriculture with energy efficiency in urban areas.
[m=6] Carol: I believe we should launch a national initiative to develop community-based microgrids powered by renewable energy, which would enhance local energy resilience and reduce transmission losses. This policy would support decarbonization by empowering communities to manage their own clean energy resources effectively.
